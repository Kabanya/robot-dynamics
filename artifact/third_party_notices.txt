Third-party software and robot descriptions

The artifact source uses Pinocchio, example-robot-data, NumPy, SciPy,
scikit-learn, Matplotlib, FFmpeg, and their transitive dependencies under their
respective licenses. Exact Python and Conda package versions are recorded in
the environment lock; video regeneration additionally requires a system FFmpeg
executable.

TALOS and iCub descriptions and meshes are loaded at runtime from
example-robot-data. They are not redistributed in this archive. This project's
BSD-3-Clause license and data license do not replace or modify any third-party
license or trademark.
