This archive contains the code and evidence for the paper's repeated
humanoid gait failure-ranking confirmation.

Contents:

- two development CSV files (100 rows each);
- two untouched confirmation CSVs (800 rows total; 400 per robot);
- all per-case checkpoints, protocols, hashes, and validity reports;
- the fixed B1--B5 analysis and paired 10,000-resample conditional bootstrap;
- the two manuscript figures, regenerated directly from the analysis JSON;
- one H.264 video showing successful and failed closed-loop rollouts for both
  robots;
- exact environment files, runnable source, checks, and license notices.

The frozen gate fails because the interval crosses zero. The paper is
self-contained; this archive adds reproducibility and visual inspection, not
supplemental claims.
