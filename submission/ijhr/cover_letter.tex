\opening{Dear Editors-in-Chief,}

Please consider the manuscript ``Whole-Body Physics Signatures for Humanoid
Gait Failure Ranking'' for publication as a Research Paper in the
\emph{International Journal of Humanoid Robotics}.

The manuscript evaluates whether normalized whole-body physics signatures add
failure-ranking information beyond gait parameters and robot identity. Fixed
models are evaluated on 800 untouched TALOS and iCub rollouts from two
independent scrambled-Sobol confirmations. The point estimate is positive on
both confirmations, but the prespecified confidence interval crosses zero and
the rollout-budget diagnostic shows no efficiency advantage. The manuscript
therefore reports the result as an inconclusive, reproducible benchmark rather
than claiming a reliable improvement, transfer result, or safety guarantee.

The work fits the journal's focus on the physical capabilities of humanoid
robots through its computational evaluation of whole-body dynamics, locomotion
feasibility, and learning-based ranking on two humanoid platforms. The
submission includes the records, fixed analysis, environment and source
fingerprints, figures, and a rollout video needed to reproduce the reported
findings.

This manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under
consideration elsewhere. I am the sole author, the work received no external
funding, and I declare no competing interests.

Thank you for considering this manuscript.

Egor Zharinov
